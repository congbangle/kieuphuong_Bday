Happy
Birthday

To the girl who makes
every ordinary moment feel like
a painting worth keeping.

scroll
Chapter I

The Beginning
of Us

Every love story starts with a single,
quiet moment neither of you plans for.

Two are better than one, because they have a good return for their labor: if either of them falls down, one can help the other up.

Ecclesiastes 4:9–10
Chapter II

Quiet
Mornings

The softest hours — coffee, light, and nowhere else to be.

This is the day the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24

"She is worth far more than rubies." Proverbs 31:10

Chapter III

Days in
the Sun

Golden light, open fields, and the warmth
she carries everywhere she goes.

The Lord bless you and keep you; the Lord make his face shine on you and be gracious to you.

Numbers 6:24–25
Chapter IV

Adventures
Together

New roads, new skies — every journey better
because she was beside me.

Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will be my people.

Ruth 1:16

"Love is patient, love is kind. It does not envy, it does not boast.
It always hopes, always perseveres." 1 Corinthians 13:4, 7

Chapter V

Small, Tender
Moments

The little things no one else notices
that I will never forget.

How beautiful you are, my darling! Oh, how beautiful! Your eyes are like doves.

Song of Solomon 1:15
Chapter VI

Her
Laughter

The sound I would travel any distance to hear.

A cheerful heart is good medicine, but a crushed spirit dries up the bones.

Proverbs 17:22

"I know the plans I have for you — plans to give you hope and a future." Jeremiah 29:11

Chapter VII

Growing
Together

Side by side, season by season
becoming more ourselves.

And now these three remain: faith, hope and love. But the greatest of these is love.

1 Corinthians 13:13
Chapter VIII

Always
& Forever

The last page of this year — and the first of the next one
we will write together.

He who finds a wife finds what is good and receives favor from the Lord.

Proverbs 18:22

"Charm is deceptive, and beauty is fleeting;
but a woman who fears the Lord is to be praised." Proverbs 31:30

Chapter IX

Her Beauty &
This Day

A chapter written only for her,
the way the light finds her,
and the way she blooms on her birthday.

You are altogether beautiful, my darling; there is no flaw in you.

Song of Solomon 4:7
